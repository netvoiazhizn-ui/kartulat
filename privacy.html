Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026 · Last updated: September 1, 2026

1. Overview

KartuLat ("we", "our", or "the app") is a free transliteration and translation service available at kartulat.com and as an Android application. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. KartuLat does not sell your personal data to third parties.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide

KartuLat does not require account registration. We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personally identifiable information directly from you.

2.2 Text you enter

When you use the translation feature, the text you enter is sent to the MyMemory translation API (mymemory.translated.net) operated by Translated SRL. We do not store the text on our servers. Please review MyMemory's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle your data.

2.3 Usage data

We may collect anonymous usage data through standard web server logs and analytics tools, including:

  • Browser type and version
  • Device type (mobile/desktop)
  • Country/region (based on IP address, not stored)
  • Pages visited and time spent

This data is used solely to improve the service and is not linked to any individual.

3. Advertising

The Android version of KartuLat uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads. You can opt out of personalised advertising in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation).

The web version (kartulat.com) may display non-personalised banner advertisements.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

KartuLat uses browser localStorage to remember your preferences (selected interface language and colour theme). This data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

We do not use tracking cookies.

5. Third-Party Services

  • MyMemory API — translation service (Privacy Policy)
  • Google AdMob — advertising (Android app) (Privacy Policy)
  • Google Fonts — typography (web) (Privacy Policy)
  • GitHub Pages — web hosting (Privacy Policy)

6. Data Retention

We do not store personal data on our servers. Anonymous usage statistics may be retained for up to 12 months for analytical purposes.

7. Children's Privacy

KartuLat is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing of your data
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

Since we collect minimal data, most of these rights are satisfied automatically. For any requests, contact us at the email below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of KartuLat after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
